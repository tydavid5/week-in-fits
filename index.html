How it works

  1. You pick a photo and build a closet from clothes you own.
  2. You pick an occasion for each day of the week, starting Monday Nov 16, 2026.
  3. The planner picks one outfit per day, with no repeats, matched to the Lagos weather and the occasion.
  4. For each day, the app sends your photo and the main garment photo to Clothsy AI through our own server, with your consent. Clothsy returns a photo of you wearing that garment. One finished try-on costs 1 Clothsy credit, and a failed try-on is refunded.
  5. Step 4 tests 3 or 4 outfits from your week on the digital mannequin, so you can see the practical outfits for those days tried on before you wear them.
  6. When no API key is set, the app runs in demo mode and shows a labelled sample try-on instead of a live Clothsy call.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo for Lagos. If the weather fetch fails, the app uses 31C, sunny, 10 percent rain, and says so on each day.
